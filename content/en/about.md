# About

I'm Yasser, a 26-year-old full-stack developer based in Lomé, Togo. I got into code the way most people do — by breaking things and wanting to understand why they broke — and I never really stopped.

Today I build web applications with Vue, Nuxt, Laravel and TypeScript. My favourite part is the moment a vague idea turns into something people actually use, and I like it even more when the code underneath is still readable six months later.

## How I got here

I studied at ESA (École Supérieure des Affaires) in Lomé and finished my Licence in 2021. My first professional role was at Golven Studio, where I built a school management application and found out what it means to ship something other people depend on.

From there I spent two years at EdPage as a full-stack developer: scalable architectures, RESTful APIs, MySQL, reusable components and state management with Pinia. I designed and built a complete CRM along the way.

Since 2025 I've been leading the technical side of a platform at JobProfils, connecting recruiters, candidates and partners.

## How I work

- **Small steps, working software.** I'd rather ship something narrow that runs than something broad that doesn't.
- **Readable over clever.** Code is read far more often than it's written, including by future me.
- **Boring tools, sharp edges.** I pick stable, well-documented technology so the interesting problems stay in the product instead of the plumbing.
- **Curiosity as a method.** I start a lot of side projects. Most go nowhere, but a few are the reason I learned something worth keeping.

## Outside the editor

I read a lot of manga, I try to work out regularly, and I'm usually halfway through some experiment. The things that aren't code live on the [shelf](/en/shelf).
