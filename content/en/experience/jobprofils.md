# Technical Project Lead at JobProfils

I lead the development of a platform that puts recruiters, candidates and partners in touch with one another. My role sits between the product and the code: shaping the architecture, carving it into the right spaces, and keeping the team moving in the same direction.

## What I worked on

- Designed the technical architecture and defined the dedicated spaces for admins, clients and partners.
- Coordinated the development team and tracked deliverables against the priorities of the project.
- Built the platform on Laravel, Nuxt and PostgreSQL.

## What I learned

That a project plan is only as good as the questions you ask before writing it. Most of the coordination work turned out to be making sure everyone meant the same thing by the same word.
