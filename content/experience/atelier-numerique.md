# Full-Stack Developer at Atelier Numérique

An agency role, which meant a new client every few months and a lot of context switching. I worked across the whole stack, from database schema to the last form validation message.

## What I worked on

- Shipped a booking platform for a network of sports centres, handling recurring schedules and cancellations.
- Replaced a spreadsheet-driven inventory process with a Laravel application that the warehouse team actually preferred using.
- Built the internal tooling for invoicing and time tracking, which is where I learned to stop underestimating the unglamorous parts.

## What I learned

How to read a business before reading the code. The useful questions were almost never about technology.
