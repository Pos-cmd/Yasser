# Web Developer at Studio Mirage

My first proper job after an apprenticeship. Small studio, small team, and a lot of freedom to touch everything — themes, plugins, deployments, and occasionally the office Wi-Fi.

## What I worked on

- Built and maintained dozens of client sites, from restaurant menus to a regional museum archive.
- Turned a recurring hand-built brochure site into a reusable theme, which shortened delivery from weeks to days.
- Handled the hosting and deployment setup, which is where I first understood what happens after `git push`.

## What I learned

That shipping something simple on time beats shipping something clever late. It sounds obvious. It was not obvious at the time.
