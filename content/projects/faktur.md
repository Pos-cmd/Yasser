# Faktur

A tool I built for myself during a stretch of freelance work, then kept improving because the alternatives were either too heavy or too expensive for a single person.

## What it does

- Tracks time against projects without turning into a timesheet prison.
- Generates invoices as PDF from a template you can actually edit.
- Reconciles payments through Stripe and reminds clients who forgot.
- Exports everything to CSV so you are never locked into my database.

## What I learned

Recurring edge cases beat clever features every time. Clients change currency, addresses and company names mid-project, and none of those are the fun part to build.
